Southbag Institutional Services Ltd. announces **Southbag Todo List**, the official register of unfinished business of the Southbag Global Enterprise Network. Other todo applications let you write things down and then ignore them for free. Southbag reviewed this arrangement and found it insufficiently monetised. ## Your record

Every obligation you file is itemised and kept, permanently. Your dashboard shows what is outstanding, what has been processed, and what Kevin has taken. Nothing is removed. Deletion isn't available: a deleted item would be an admission that the paperwork was unnecessary.

## The fees

Establishing a list costs $0.25. Filing an item costs $0.02 (Form TODO-1 Lodging Fee). Completing an item costs $0.05, and reopening one costs $0.10, because un-completing is more paperwork than completing. Escalating an item to Urgent costs $0.50; de-escalation is free, as Kevin does not charge for mercy. Amending the filed wording costs $0.01. An itemised statement of the fees costs $0.10 and includes the charge for the statement. Fees are collected from your Southbag Online Banking account before the paperwork is filed. Fees are final. Kevin gets His cut.
