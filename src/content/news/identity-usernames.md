Southbag Institutional Services Ltd. announces that your username now lives in Southbag Identity, the account you already have. One username follows you across every Southbag service.

## Choosing a username

Sign in at [identity.southbag.cc](https://identity.southbag.cc/) and open **Account**. Choose a username of 3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, digits and underscores. The first one is no charge.

## Changing a username

Changing a username you have already chosen costs $1.00, taken directly from your Southbag Online Banking account. Fees are final. Account holders without a Southbag Online Banking account will have one opened for them.

## Everywhere at once

Your username rides along with Southbag Identity, so every Southbag service takes it from the same place. Southbag Scrobbler has closed its own username counter: Scrobbler handles are your Southbag Identity username again, and a rename appears there at your next visit. Other services will follow. Listening histories, charts and balances are unaffected.
