Southbag Institutional Services Ltd. announces **Southbag Vault**, the password manager of the Southbag Global Enterprise Network. Other password managers encrypt your passwords, so that nobody can read them. Southbag reviewed this arrangement and found it insufficiently legible. Southbag Vault stores everything in plaintext: fully readable, fully auditable, permanently on file. It is available now at vault.southbag.cc. Sign in with Southbag Identity. You already have an account.

## Your vault

Every credential you lodge is stored exactly as typed and kept forever. Your dashboard shows what is on file, what has been inspected, and what Kevin has taken. Nothing is removed. Deletion isn't available: a deleted password would be an admission it was never valid.

## The fees

Amending the filed wording costs $0.05. Exporting the whole vault, exactly as lodged, costs $1.00 (Bulk Legibility Fee). A Comprehensive Security Audit costs $2.00 and concludes, every time, that the vault is operating exactly as chartered. Retrieving the index of your own paperwork costs $0.01; refreshing the page buys it again. Fees are collected from your Southbag Online Banking account before the paperwork is filed. Fees are final. Kevin gets His cut.
