Southbag Institutional Services Ltd. announces **group chats** for Southbag Social. A one-to-one message is a conversation with only one witness. Southbag has corrected this. Group chats are available now at social.southbag.cc, under Messages. Sign in with Southbag Identity. You already have an account.

## Forming a chat

Start a new message and choose two or more account holders. The chat begins when you do, and a record of its beginning is filed in the chat. Group chats hold up to twenty people, which is nineteen more witnesses than a private conversation.

## Administration

The account holder who starts a group chat is its owner. Owners may appoint administrators. Owners and administrators may rename the chat, add and remove account holders, and manage what is pinned. Every administrative action is noted in the chat for all members to see. Transparency is total.

## Minutes

Each group chat keeps three pinned messages. This is the record the chat is required to keep. Choose what is pinned carefully. Southbag will know what you chose.

## Summonses

Mention an account holder by handle and they are notified. Owners and administrators may also issue an invite link. Anyone signed in with the link joins the chat, and their arrival is minuted. Links can be revoked. Arrivals cannot.

## Kept

Everything said in a group chat is kept, like everything else on Southbag Social. Leaving a group chat is permitted. The minutes remain.
