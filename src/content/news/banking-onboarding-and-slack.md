Southbag has simplified account opening. Completing onboarding at [southbag.cc/onboarding](/onboarding) now opens a Southbag Banking account and places the new customer directly on their banking dashboard. There is no second form. There is no waiting period. Kevin considers the matter settled.

## How it works

Onboarding creates a Southbag Identity, signs the customer in, and hands them to [banking.southbag.cc](https://banking.southbag.cc). The banking account is opened on arrival. Visitors who already hold a signed-in Southbag session skip the forms entirely and are sent straight through.

For administrative consistency, every new account is registered to "Southbag Customer". The name collected during onboarding is retained for Kevin's records. It is not used for anything else. Kevin knows who you are regardless.

## Banking with Slack

A dedicated flow is available for customers who would like Southbag Banking connected to Slack. It begins with a single question: *do you already have a Southbag account?*

- **Yes** — sign in with existing Southbag Identity credentials.
- **No** — complete the standard registration steps.

Either way, the customer is signed into Southbag Banking and taken directly into Sign in with Slack to link their workspace. Kevin has been present in Slack for some time. Your account may now join Him.
